1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Dat Luyen, the individual developer of the app ("we", "us" or "our"), collects, uses, stores and protects information when you use the AI Room Design & Decor mobile application, this website and the related AI services (together, the "Services").

By using the Services, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Services.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the information we need to provide and improve the Services:

  • Device information: device model, operating system version, app version and device or advertising identifiers.
  • Usage data: features you use, styles you select, session length and in-app interactions.
  • Diagnostics: crash logs and performance data.
  • Network information: IP address and approximate region derived from it.
  • Settings: language and region preferences.
  • User content: room, home, garden and floor plan photos, sketches, floor plans you create, prompts, and the designs generated from them.
  • Optional personal details: the birth date you choose to enter for personalized decor suggestions.
  • Purchase information: subscription status provided by Apple. We do not receive or store your payment card details.

3. AI Content Processing

To create redesigns, 3D renders and edits, the content you submit is sent securely to our servers or our AI processing providers. This may include:

  • Photos of rooms, outdoor spaces and gardens
  • Floor plan images, blueprints and sketches
  • Selected style templates, room types and text prompts
  • Generated images and edited output

Your content is processed only to produce the results you request. We do not sell your content and do not share it with advertisers.

4. Photo & Media Uploads

When you upload a photo or choose one from your library, the file is transferred to our servers only to generate the content you requested. Uploaded files are deleted automatically from our servers within 24 hours of processing. Designs you save to your project library are stored on your device unless you choose to share or export them.

We access your camera or photo library only after you grant permission, and only when you choose to take or pick a photo. You can change these permissions at any time in your device settings.

5. User Consent for AI Processing

We tell you before your content is sent for AI processing. By uploading content and tapping a generate or edit action, you consent to that content being processed as described in this policy.

5.1 Photos That Contain People

The app is built to design spaces, not people. If a photo you upload happens to include people or faces:

  • We do not use it to identify, recognize or track anyone.
  • We do not extract biometric or facial features.
  • We do not share it with third parties except our processing providers acting on our behalf.
  • It is deleted from our servers within 24 hours.
  • It is not used to train public AI models.

5.2 Birth Date for Personalized Suggestions

Entering your birth date is optional. If you provide it, we use it only to generate personalized decor suggestions such as colors and arrangements. It is not used for advertising and is not shared with third parties. You can remove it from the app at any time.

6. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Generate redesigns, edits, floor plans and 3D renders you request
  • Provide, maintain and operate the Services
  • Manage subscriptions and unlock Premium features
  • Show advertising to free users and measure its performance
  • Improve performance, quality and reliability
  • Prevent fraud, abuse and violations of our Terms of Use
  • Troubleshoot problems and respond to support requests
  • Keep the Services secure
  • Comply with legal obligations

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share limited information only with:

  • Service providers: cloud hosting, storage, AI processing, analytics, crash reporting and security providers who process data on our behalf under contractual confidentiality and data-protection obligations.
  • Advertising partners: the free version contains ads. Ad partners may receive device or advertising identifiers to serve and measure ads. On iOS, we ask for your permission through App Tracking Transparency before any tracking, and you can change this in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Premium subscribers do not see ads.
  • Apple: purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy.
  • Legal requirements: when required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and property of our users, the public or us.

8. Data Retention

  • Uploaded photos, sketches and prompts are deleted from our servers automatically within 24 hours.
  • Generated designs are deleted from our servers within 24 hours after they are delivered to your device.
  • Saved projects stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app.
  • Diagnostics and usage data are kept only as long as needed for the purposes in this policy, then deleted or anonymized.
  • We may keep some information longer if the law requires it.

9. Data Deletion Requests

You can delete saved projects directly in the app. To ask us to delete any other data related to you, email support@mindwaveai.store with the subject "Data Deletion Request". We will confirm and complete your request within 30 days.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including encrypted transfer (HTTPS/TLS) and access controls, to protect your information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Children's Privacy

The Services are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

12. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information
  • Restrict or object to certain processing
  • Receive your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details below. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the new version on this page and update the "Last Updated" date. If the changes are significant, we will also notify you in the app.
