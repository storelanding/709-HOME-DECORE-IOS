Frequently asked questions

Which devices are supported?

AI Room Design & Decor needs iOS 15.0 or later on iPhone and iPod touch, macOS 12.0 or later on Macs with an Apple M1 chip or later, or visionOS 1.0 or later on Apple Vision Pro.

What kind of photos work best?

Bright, well-lit photos taken straight on, with most of the room in frame, give the most realistic results. Avoid heavy blur or extreme wide-angle distortion.

What happens to the photos I upload?

Your photos are only used to generate the designs you ask for. Uploaded files are deleted from our servers automatically within 24 hours. Read our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose AI Room Design & Decor. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid being charged for the next period.

Can I use the designs for my real renovation?

Yes. The designs are meant for inspiration and planning. AI-generated images are visualizations, so check sizes, materials and structural details with a professional before you build.
